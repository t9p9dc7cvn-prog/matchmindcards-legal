Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Memorize the board while the cards are face up, then tap two cards at a time to find matching pairs. Each try uses one move — find every pair before you run out. You can replay the tutorial any time in Settings → How to Play.

How are stars awarded?
3 stars for at most 1.5 tries per pair, 2 stars for at most 2.25, and 1 star for any other clear. Replay a level to improve your stars.

How do I unlock the next world?
Earn 20 stars in a world to unlock the next one. The Levels tab shows your progress toward each unlock.

What is the Daily Challenge?
One board per day, the same layout for every player. Finishing it keeps your daily streak going and unlocks the daily spin.

Does MatchMind need an internet connection?
No. All gameplay works offline. An internet connection is only used for ads, Game Center leaderboards and to check for app updates.

Does MatchMind show ads?
MatchMind is free. You can choose to watch a short video for extra moves, a Peek or a bigger daily spin, and an occasional short ad may appear between levels — never during play, in the tutorial or in the Daily Challenge.

How do the leaderboards work?
If you are signed in to Game Center, your Daily Challenge score (fewest moves wins) and your total stars appear on its leaderboards. Open them from the Progress tab or from the Daily Challenge result. If Game Center is off, turn it on in iOS Settings → Game Center.

A video didn't play — did I lose my reward?
If no video is available, the game gives you a small free reward instead, once per attempt. Videos only reward you when watched to the end.

How do I reset my progress?
Open Settings (gear icon on the Today screen) and choose “Reset All Progress”. Your settings are kept.

Is my data backed up anywhere?
Your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or switching devices without an iCloud/device backup, resets your levels and scores. Game Center achievements and leaderboard scores stay with your Game Center account.

How do I change sound, haptics, appearance or reminders?
All are in Settings. Relaxed mode (no timer and no move limit) is there too.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.
Please email us — screenshots and steps to reproduce a bug are always appreciated.
