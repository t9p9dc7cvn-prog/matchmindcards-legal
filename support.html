Frequently asked questions

Does MatchMind need an internet connection?
No. MatchMind works completely offline — no account, no sign-in, no network access required.

How do I reset my progress?
Open Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and use "Reset Progress" for the difficulty you want to reset. You can also long-press a difficulty on the home screen for the same option.

Is my data backed up anywhere?
Your progress is stored only on your device. Reinstalling the app or switching devices without an iCloud/device backup will reset your saved levels and scores.

How do I turn sound or dark mode on/off?
Both are in Settings — "Sound Effects" toggles the audio, and "Appearance" lets you lock MatchMind to Light or Dark regardless of your phone's system setting.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.
Please email us — screenshots and steps to reproduce a bug are always appreciated.
