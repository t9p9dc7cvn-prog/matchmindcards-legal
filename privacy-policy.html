MatchMind — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
The short version: your game progress stays on your device. MatchMind has no accounts and no analytics. The app shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob; Google may process device data to deliver and measure those ads, and in regions that require it you are asked for consent first.

Data stored on your device

MatchMind saves your levels, stars, best scores, daily results, streak, play statistics, any paused game, and your settings (sound, haptics, appearance, relaxed mode, reminders) on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset All Progress, or by deleting the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MatchMind is free and shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, for extra moves or a Peek). There are no ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

  • To show, cap and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads still work but are less personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are shown a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. Where this applies, you can review or change your choice in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Other network access

  • Update check: the app asks Apple's App Store whether a newer version of MatchMind is available. No personal information is included in this request.
  • Everything else, including all gameplay, works offline.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, MatchMind schedules a local notification on your device — at most one per day. No notification server is used and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

What MatchMind does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • It does not sell your personal information.

Children's privacy

MatchMind is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app shows ads, it is intended for a general audience.

Changes to this policy

If MatchMind's data practices change, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change.
