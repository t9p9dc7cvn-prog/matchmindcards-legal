MatchMind — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
The short version: MatchMind does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline.

What MatchMind does on your device

MatchMind saves your game progress — level, best move count, best time, win streaks, and your chosen theme, sound, and appearance preferences — directly on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. None of this ever leaves your phone.

What MatchMind does not do

  • It does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not connect to the internet or make any network requests.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information.
  • It does not share any data with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

Because MatchMind collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of any age, including children.

Changes to this policy

If MatchMind's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
